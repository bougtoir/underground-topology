## Reading

- The paper's *natural* citation neighborhood (power-distribution planning journals) is
  currently gated: the flagship Elsevier planning venue (IJEPES) went Gold OA in 2024, EPSR
  and TPWRD are JCR Q2 in 2024 JIF, and IEEE TPWRS carries mandatory overlength charges with a
  10-page submission limit that the current manuscript cannot meet at zero cost.
- The *viable* neighborhood is infrastructure systems / asset management and applied
  planning/OR: CAIE, RESS, JME, TUST, C&IE, SEPS, CEUS, SCS all (a) publish
  computational network-planning or life-cycle infrastructure investment studies and
  (b) offer a subscription route with zero mandatory author charges at confirmed JCR Q1.
- Reference additions justified by actual overlap (not journal-fitting): Larsen 2016 and
  Fenrick & Getachew 2012 anchor the undergrounding-economics frame; Samui 2012 and
  Kumar 2014 are the closest feeder-routing predecessors; Abhilash/Bandam et al. 2021 is the
  closest synthetic-topology reconstruction precedent. All five materially support the
  Introduction/Discussion comparisons; no citation was added to decorate scope.
