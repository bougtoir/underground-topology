# 04 — JIF/JCR and fee evidence (accessed 2026-10-10 UTC)

JCR = Journal Citation Reports, JIF reporting year 2024 (released June 2025); "2025 JIF"
figures shown by aggregators (released June 2026) are noted where seen. Direct JCR access is
paywalled; quartiles below are corroborated by at least two independent secondary sources
(journalmetrics.org — Clarivate-based records; iit.comillas.edu JCR-JIF table;
dizin.docent.com.tr JCR data; EURO official ranking statement for EJOR). Fee facts are quoted
from official publisher pages.

## ELIGIBLE (Q1 + zero mandatory author charge on a usable route)

- **Computer-Aided Civil and Infrastructure Engineering** — JIF 9.1 (JCR 2024), Q1
  (Engineering, Civil: rank 2/183; also Q1 CS Interdisc. & Transportation Sci. & Tech.).
  Sources: journalsimpactfactors.com record; wos-journal.info ("Hybrid and Open Access
  Support"); Wiley journal page. Wiley hybrid → subscription route carries no APC; no
  submission fee found in GFA. Verified Q1: secondary-confirmed.
- **Reliability Engineering & System Safety** — JIF 11.0 (2024), Q1 Engineering Industrial +
  Q1 OR&MS (journalmetrics.org; bioxbio JIF table). Official ScienceDirect page:
  "Subscription — No publication fee charged to authors"; OA APC USD 4,590 optional.
- **Sustainable Cities and Society** — JIF 12.0 (2024), Q1 (iit.comillas JCR table;
  journalmetrics). ScienceDirect: subscription route "No publication fee charged to authors".
- **Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology** — JIF 7.4 (2024), Q1 Construction &
  Building Tech + Q1 Engineering Civil (journalmetrics; dizin JIF rank 10/184). ScienceDirect:
  subscription "No publication fee"; OA APC USD 3,630 optional.
- **Computers, Environment and Urban Systems** — JIF 8.3 (2024), Q1 SSCI (dizin: rank 5/174).
  ScienceDirect: subscription "No publication fee".
- **Computers & Industrial Engineering** — JIF 6.5 (2024), Q1 CS Interdisc. + Q1 Engineering
  Industrial (journalmetrics). ScienceDirect: "No publication fee charged to authors".
- **Socio-Economic Planning Sciences** — JIF 5.4 (2024), Q1 (journalmetrics). ScienceDirect:
  subscription "No publication fee"; OA APC USD 3,590 optional.
- **European Journal of Operational Research** — JIF 6.0 (2024), Q1 OR&MS rank 14/106
  (EURO official statement, euro-online.org). ScienceDirect: subscription "No publication
  fee"; EURO page confirms OA is optional (author-paid only if selected).
- **Omega** — JIF 7.2 (2024), Q1 Management + Q1 OR&MS (journalmetrics; meizhang JCR
  ranks). Elsevier hybrid; subscription route no fee.
- **Decision Support Systems** — JIF 6.8 (2024), Q1 (3 categories; journalmetrics).
  ScienceDirect: subscription "No publication fee"; OA APC USD 3,900 optional.
- **Cities** — JIF 6.6 (2024), Q1 (journalmetrics). ScienceDirect: subscription "No
  publication fee".
- **Applied Energy** — JIF 11.0 (2024), Q1 (sciencedirect banner; journalsearches Q1 record).
  ScienceDirect: subscription "No publication fee"; OA APC USD 4,210 optional.
- **Journal of Management in Engineering (ASCE)** — JIF 7.0 (2024), Q1 Engineering Civil
  rank 13/183 (journalsimpactfactors; ivysci Q1 Civil). ASCE author center: technical papers
  ≤30 double-spaced pages; no submission fee or mandatory page charge found; ASCE OA optional.

## INELIGIBLE

- **IJEPES** — JIF 5.0 (2024), Q1. Elsevier official notice: "On 01 January 2024, IJEPES will
  become a fully gold open access title"; articles submitted after 31 Aug 2023 pay APC USD
  4,190. (sciencedirect.com/.../about/news/...transitioning-to-gold-open-access)
- **Energy Economics** — JIF 14.2 (2024), Q1 Economics. Mandatory non-refundable submission
  fee USD 100 (USD 50 students) per GFA; corroborated by Elsevier submission-fee policy page
  (~50 economics titles levy submission fees).
- **Electric Power Systems Research** — JIF 4.2 (2024), JCR Q2 (journalmetrics). Hybrid but
  fails Q1 gate.
- **IEEE Transactions on Power Delivery** — JIF 3.7/4.7 (2024/2025), JCR Q2 EE
  (journalmetrics; iit.comillas). Also overlength charges beyond 8 pages.
- **Utilities Policy** — JIF 4.4 (2024), JCR Q2 (iit.comillas; journalmetrics).
- **Sustainable Energy Technologies and Assessments** — JIF 7.0 (2024), JCR Q2
  (iit.comillas; journalmetrics).
- **Journal of Infrastructure Systems** — JIF 3.8, JCR Q2 (wos-journal.info percentile 72.5).
- **IET GTD / ITEES / MPCE / CSEE JPES / Energies / Int. J. Energy Research** — gold-OA
  mandatory APC or below-Q1; excluded without further audit.
- **IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid** — JIF 9.8 Q1, but scope requires smart-grid operational
  performance (same critique as SEGAN) → excluded on scope, not fees.
- **IEEE Transactions on Power Systems** — JIF 8.7 Q1. IEEE PES author rules (official):
  ≤10 pages at submission; **mandatory $250/page beyond 12 published pages**; voluntary
  $110/page. A ~9,000-word+figures manuscript in two-column format plausibly exceeds 12
  published pages → zero-fee not guaranteed → NEEDS VERIFICATION, not recommended.

## Caveats

- Quartiles are secondary-source confirmed (iit.comillas / journalmetrics / dizin / EURO);
  none contradict. Mark UNVERIFIED only where a single aggregator was the sole source — none
  of the recommended candidates rely on single-source quartiles.
- Elsevier hybrid pages quote OA APC alongside the free subscription line; APC applies only
  if the author selects OA — none of the Elsevier/ASCE/Wiley hybrid titles above levy
  submission or page fees on the traditional route.
