# CITATION ECOLOGY AND NOVELTY AUDIT

## Verdict: GO (reposition) — real but bounded increment

### Overlap analysis (from 02_CLOSEST_PRIOR_WORK_MATRIX.csv)

- Undergrounding economics exists (Larsen 2016 Energy Economics; Fenrick & Getachew 2012
  Utilities Policy; NYS PSC 2023; Infrastructure Victoria 2023) but evaluates
  underground-vs-overhead *system policy*, never the topology choice *inside* a committed
  underground renewal program.
- Feeder routing / expansion planning (Samui 2012; Kumar 2014; Soft Computing 2023; Ayazi
  2021; Camargo 2013) optimizes greenfield/expansion topology without an
  incumbent-admissible choice set or a renewal-window option-value estimand.
- Synthetic topology generation (Abhilash/Bandam 2021; synthetic grid generator 2024)
  produces plausible incumbent proxies — our reconstruction ensemble is directly comparable
  — but stops at synthesis; it does not evaluate redesign value or feasibility gating.
- No located prior work computes a nonnegative option value of redesign over a
  reconstruction ensemble with LV feasibility gating. Increment confirmed at the
  estimand/measurement level; algorithmic core is standard (as disclosed).
