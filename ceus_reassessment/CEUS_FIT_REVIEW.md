# Gate 5 — CEUS fit reassessment (four skeptical lenses)

## 1. CEUS geospatial/urban-systems editor lens
- Does new understanding of urban systems emerge? Partially: the paper adds a *decision layer* on top of reconstructed hidden infrastructure — the option value of re-layout at renewal — and shows the value is bounded and spatially structured. That is a geospatial decision-support contribution in CEUS's declared application area ("infrastructure and facilities management", "decision support systems"). It is not a new urban theory.
- Is the spatial angle substantive or cosmetic? With Gate-3 morphology analysis it is substantive but modest: cluster-level descriptors show a consistent, interpretable concentration of flexibility value in larger/sparser clusters. Without it, the paper would be engineering-only → cosmetic. With it, the geospatial linkage is real but descriptive.

## 2. GIS/spatial-statistics lens
- Three zones + clustered clusters: acceptable only as descriptive case analysis — which is what the draft claims. Morphology metrics follow established canon (degree, edge length, hull area, span, density). CRS handling verified (projected metres). Snap distances disclosed. LOZO/predictive validation correctly declined (n=3 zones).
- Weakness that must stay explicit: convenience zones, OSM completeness unverified, reconstruction is a model not an observation.

## 3. Infrastructure/OR/engineering lens
- Feasibility gating + incumbent-admissible estimand is a defensible methodological contribution for renewal planning; AC validation on synthesized feeders is adequate for a screening-level claim.
- The bounded VoF (<0.5% LCC) is a legitimate negative-ish result with decision value (like-for-like renewal defensible), not a failure of the model.

## 4. Reproducibility/bibliography lens
- All 18 audited references verified via Crossref/official records except Liu/TNO (full text accessible; DOI partial — cited with repository record) and the Synthetic Grid Generator preprint (TechRxiv DOI). No fabricated records. Numbers all trace to machine-readable outputs. Single-authored; declarations drafted.

## Common caveat across lenses
- Transferability: findings are three modeled Japanese zones; the transferable artifact is the *method + screening rule*, not the magnitudes. Manuscript must not claim Japan-wide or general-utility conclusions.

## Decision

**PASS — CEUS-focused drafting permitted**, conditional on language discipline: descriptive morphology results, zero-inflation disclosure, modeled-network framing, no smart-grid framing, screening-level conclusion.
