## Consequences for the manuscript package

1. The previous (JME/CEUS) layout with figures and tables **embedded in the main text** conflicts with ASCE rules 13–14. Following the instruction to preserve integration *unless official requirements require otherwise*, the JIS submission DOCX places figure captions after References, tables after the caption list, and supplies figures as separate files. A review-convenience PDF with in-text figures is **not** produced, to avoid two divergent versions.
2. Numbered section headings are removed (rule 8).
3. References are converted to ASCE author–date (rule 12).
4. The abstract is lengthened to 250–300 words and contains no equations or citations (rule 6); a 150–200-word Practical Applications section is added (rule 7).
5. Data Availability Statement uses ASCE's verbatim options (rule 18).
6. No paid OA, no print colour, no charge of any kind is selected (rules 15, 21–23). Any change requires explicit author authorization.
